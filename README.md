## 🚀 Featured work

### 1. PulseFlow Studio
A modular workflow dashboard that orchestrates automation tasks, tracks execution health, and surfaces actionable alerts across multiple service pipelines.

- Built for orchestration, rapid iteration, and operational visibility
- Combined event-driven patterns with a clean UI for monitoring and recovery
- Reduced time to diagnose workflow issues through structured logs and state tracking

Tech: `TypeScript` `React` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`

### 2. SignalBoard
A lightweight reporting and insights platform for summarizing operational metrics into easy-to-digest views for product and operations teams.

- Simplified decision-making with real-time dashboards and trend snapshots
- Focused on clarity, accessibility, and low-friction interactions
- Designed to make recurring operational reporting easier to maintain and share

Tech: `Python` `FastAPI` `React` `Redis` `SQL`

### 3. DeckPilot
An internal tool for preparing structured documentation and deployment checklists, reducing duplication and improving handoff quality across teams.

- Centralized project preparation workflows
- Standardized operational reviews and release readiness checks
- Improved consistency across planning, release, and rollout tasks

Tech: `JavaScript` `Express` `GitHub Actions` `MongoDB`
